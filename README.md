# FormatDaddy  
*“Your assignment’s final touch.”*  

---

**FormatDaddy** automatically formats your `.docx` assignments to match college submission guidelines, fonts, spacing, margins, everything, in one click.

Tired of wrestling with Word formatting before every submission?  

**FormatDaddy** takes your uploaded `.docx` file and instantly applies your desired formatting.  
No installations. No backend. Just:  
**Upload → Set your preferences → Download your perfectly formatted document.**  

Built entirely on the client side using **Next.js**, **Mammoth.js**, and **docx.js**, so your files **never leave your browser**.

---

## Core Features  
-  **Upload `.docx` file** (drag & drop or file picker)  
-  **Input formatting parameters** (margins, font, spacing, alignment)  
-  **Instant reformatting**, 100% client-side  
-  **Download the new `.docx`** instantly  
-  **Privacy-first:** no server, no upload, no data stored  

---

##  How It Works  
1. **Upload** your unformatted `.docx` file.  
2. **Choose** the formatting you need (college preset or custom inputs).  
3. **Click “Format”** — FormatDaddy processes everything locally in your browser.  
4. **Download** your ready-to-submit document.  

>  All processing is done **client-side** — your data never leaves your device.
